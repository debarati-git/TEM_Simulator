# v4.9.18 — Pre-Start Exploration Mode

Based on v4.9.17 Section 2 Step 3 compact modal build.

- On microscope-new.html, **before Start** show two labeled hotspots on the TEM photograph for L1 and R1.
- Hotspots open the original JEOL left/right control-panel photographs already bundled under assets/images/sop/section3-l1-modal-reference.png and section3-r1-modal-reference.png, in the existing control locator modal without a step-specific highlight.
- Mouse wheel over the pre-start column image zooms between 100% and 300%, focused on the mouse pointer. At zoom > 100%, pointer drag pans and double-click resets. +/- and Reset are available as buttons.
- On Start, hide exploration, reset zoom/pan, close the photo modal, and run existing sample selection and SOP unchanged.
- Restart returns to the pre-start exploration view.
- Section 3 control-location highlighting continues to use its original image modal behavior.

Note: Pre-start mode never updates microscopy settings, status, or SOP completion.
