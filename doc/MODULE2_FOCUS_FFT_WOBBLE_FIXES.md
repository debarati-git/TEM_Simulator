# Module 2 Focus, FFT and Wobble Fixes

## Changes

1. Objective focus now requires the learner to operate Coarse Focus first and then Fine Focus. A later coarse movement invalidates the prior fine adjustment and requires Fine Focus again.
2. The camera histogram was removed. Raising the fluorescent screen automatically scrolls the camera drawer to the Live FFT palette, which is briefly highlighted.
3. The wobble amplitude meter, ghost images and animation are shown only during the active eucentric-height Z-adjustment step and are cleared immediately after the eucentric condition is reached.
