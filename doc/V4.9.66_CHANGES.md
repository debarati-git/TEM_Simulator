# TEM Simulator v4.9.66 — Section 3 Step 12 specimen navigation

Derived directly from v4.9.65 (whose base is the attached v4.9.62).

- Step 12 starts on the existing Tv2 image (200×).
- Stage-trackball movement advances through Tv1 (500×), Tv5 (8000×), and Tv4 (8000×).
- Visual progression is travel-driven and rate-limited to make all four frames observable, even when the stage trackball is moved quickly.
- Existing specimen images are pixel-identical to the TIFF files supplied by the user; no re-encoding or replacement of images was needed.
- Final Tv4 remains in Step 13 and the following initial imaging steps to prevent an image jump on step transition.
- The trackball does not silently modify the R1 MAG/CAM L setting, because stage movement and magnification controls are physically separate.
- Skip/Undo/navigation and all non-Section-3 Step-12 interfaces are preserved.
