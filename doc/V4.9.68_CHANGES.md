# TEM Simulator v4.9.68 — Section 3 Refinements

Based on v4.9.67 (which itself was derived from the user's v4.9.66 approved baseline). All untouched assets and sections are preserved.

1. **Section 3 Step 3 — Auto HT duration:** If the calculated ramp exceeds 20 minutes, a locked SOP confirmation modal appears with the actual calculated time and an OK acknowledgement. The learner revises Step and/or Time/Step; an over-limit choice cannot advance the step. Any combination yielding exactly 20 minutes remains valid for Step 4.
2. **Steps 2–3 — TEMCON:** Removed the instructional 20-minute advice card from inside the TEMCON image. The live calculated numeric **Time to finish** readout remains displayed. The explanatory guidance remains in the regular instruction bar and the warning modal only.
3. **Step 17 — Magnification:** Moving the mouse over or clicking MAG/CAM L does not swap the specimen photograph. A deliberate drag/rotary change or keyboard adjustment initiates a 2.7-second smooth zoom/crossfade from the preceding Tv4 view to Tv6; completion at X40k waits until the image has settled.
4. **Step 18 — Crossover:** The learner must turn BRIGHTNESS to the far spreading side (at least 75 on the simulated 0–100 scale), then return to the midpoint (within 3 units of 50) and hold that position briefly. The beam spreads on both sides of the midpoint.
5. **Step 22 — Wobble:** Until MAG WOB is clicked, the Viewing Screen retains the Step 20 specimen composition (Tv7). Once clicked, registered underfocus and overfocus frames derived from the same Tv7 photographic field alternate while the specimen wobbles, with no angular or crop jump. On minimizing wobble near Z = 250 µm, the specified original Tv9 remains the final image. The uploaded Tv9 focus photographs remain in the archive unchanged, but are not swapped into Step 22 because they show different framing.
