# v4.9.77 — Section 4 Shutdown adjustments

Based on v4.9.76 S3-integrated simulator.

1. Re-entering Section 4 resets its completion and skipped-step state and begins again from Step 1.
2. Section 4 Step 1 shows TEMCON's top status area with a blank lower pane, using the existing top-right Neutralize button.
3. Neutralize is highlighted in orange until clicked and the stage coordinates are zeroed.
4. Step 3 starts in MAG 1 at X60k with MAG/CAM L at the high end; selecting LOW MAG activates the knob and moving it to X5k or below completes the step.
5. Step 4 starts with the Beam indicator dark green. Pressing BEAM on L1 turns the lamp gray and labels the beam NotReady as current goes to zero.
6. Step 6 automatically scrolls TEMCON to the top after reaching 80 kV.
7. Step 12 displays the user-supplied Bake Out / ACD Heat image directly from its S3 URL. The ON region is interactive.
8. Step 13 now uses confirmation-only logbook acknowledgement. No CSV or logbook download is generated.

The existing 53 local image files and all prior S3 URLs are retained unchanged. No local copy of the new S3-hosted ACD screenshot is added.
