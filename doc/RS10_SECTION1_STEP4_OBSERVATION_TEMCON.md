# RS10 — Section 1 Step 4 TEMCON observation refinement

- Section 1 Step 4 is now an observation-only readiness check.
- HT Ready, Evac Ready, and 80.00 kV are displayed as live visual readouts; the learner is not required to click individual boxes.
- The TEMCON PC drawer opens first and remains unobstructed for 7 seconds before the confirmation modal appears.
- The confirmation modal is dismissible like other SOP step dialogs. If the learner closes it, **Show Step** reopens it immediately without rebuilding or losing the TEMCON screen state.
- During this step the PC drawer fills the viewer stage. The 1280×1024 TEMCON reconstruction is automatically fitted horizontally so High Voltage Control and Valve Status are visible together at opening.
- Horizontal and vertical scrollbars remain available to inspect the full reconstructed desktop.
- The drawer can still be collapsed and reopened without losing the Step 4 TEMCON state.
- RS9 TEMCON menu interactions and RS8 phosphor/camera behavior remain preserved.
